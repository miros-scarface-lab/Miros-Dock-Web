Two powerful modes. One tiny dock.

Everything you need to move files.

01 ↔

Blazing P2P Transfer

Send files directly over your local Wi-Fi or hotspot at full hardware speeds. No file size limits and zero mobile data used.

02 ⌗

Portable Web Dock

Turn your phone or PC into an instant local web server. Anyone on your network can browse and download files using only a web browser.

03 ❖

Instant QR Pairing

Connect devices effortlessly with automatic local network discovery (mDNS) or scan a QR code on-screen to start transferring in seconds.

04 ▣

Yours Stays Yours

Your transfers are protected end-to-end and never pass through cloud servers. Secure your Web Dock with customizable PIN access.

05 ↻

Picks Up Where It Left Off

A wobbly connection never means starting over. Miros Dock's chunked engine automatically resumes transfers right where they paused.

06 ↑

Stream & Two-Way Upload

Stream videos and audio directly within any browser, or upload files from secondary devices straight back to your host dock.
